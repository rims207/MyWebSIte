Shipping & delivery

We aim to make delivery simple, reliable, and transparent for customers across Pakistan.

Coverage

SmartShoes delivers to major cities and regions across Pakistan. Final delivery availability and fees should be kept in sync with the live courier arrangements used by the business.

Processing time

Orders are usually prepared within 1–3 working days, depending on product availability and stock confirmation.

Delivery charges

  • Free shipping on qualifying orders above the configured threshold
  • Standard delivery charges apply for smaller orders
  • Cash on Delivery orders may include a COD fee depending on policy

Address accuracy

To avoid delays, customers should provide a complete and accurate delivery address with the correct city, area, and contact number. Failed deliveries or redelivery attempts should be handled according to the actual courier policy in effect.
