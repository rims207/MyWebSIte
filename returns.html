Returns & exchanges

We aim to make returns and exchanges fair, clear, and easy to understand.

Eligibility

Items may be eligible for return or exchange if they are unworn, unused, and returned in their original packaging, subject to the current business policy.

Damaged or wrong item

If the item is damaged upon delivery or differs from the order, contact support immediately with photos and order details for review.

Important policy note

Actual return windows, responsibility for shipping costs, and refund timelines should be confirmed against the official SmartShoes business policy before publishing.
