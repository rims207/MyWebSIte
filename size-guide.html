Find your perfect fit

Use these general guidelines to estimate your size before ordering. Exact fit may vary slightly by shoe type.

How to measure

  1. Stand on a piece of paper with your heel against a wall.
  2. Mark the furthest point of your foot.
  3. Measure the length in centimeters.
  4. Match your measurement to the table below.

Fit notes

Sports and running shoes often fit slightly snugger. Casual and formal styles can feel a bit more structured. The best fit usually allows a tiny bit of comfort room at the toe.

If you are between sizes

If you are uncertain, choose the larger size for everyday comfort or contact support for the exact product recommendation.
